Humidity Intelligence

Humidity Intelligence acts as a stabiliser for your home environment. It uses data from Home Assistant smart sensors to help guide air purifiers, extractors, humidifiers, and dehumidifiers, so your home can stay fresher, calmer, and more comfortable day to day.

Why it exists

Because it matters more than ever.

With global temperatures rising and warmer, more humid weather patterns becoming more common across parts of the Northern Hemisphere, indoor humidity is becoming harder to ignore. In many homes, conditions that once shifted slowly with the seasons can now change in a single day: damp air after showers, cooking, or warm weather, then drier air when heating, cooling, or ventilation takes over.

Those swings can affect more than comfort. Humidity that sits too high or too low may contribute to dry skin, irritated eyes, coughing, respiratory sensitivity, stressed houseplants, condensation, mould risk, and, over time, damage to the fabric of a home. Heat and humidity together can also put extra strain on the body, because humid air makes it harder for sweat to evaporate and cool us down.

Humidity Intelligence does not replace good ventilation, heating, cooling, or maintenance. It helps make the indoor picture easier to understand by bringing humidity, temperature, dew point, room behaviour, air quality, and device states together inside Home Assistant, then explaining what is changing, why it matters, and what the next sensible action may be to support a more stable indoor environment.

What it helps with

Practical guidance for fresh air, comfort, and damp-risk awareness.

One clear recommendation

When the home sends mixed signals, HI follows a fixed priority order: CO emergency, humidity danger, mould danger, mould risk, condensation danger, condensation risk, Zone 1, Zone 2, air quality, then normal operation. You get one clear control decision instead of overlapping automation noise.

Season-aware comfort

Winter and summer homes need different humidity targets. HI reads each room against the active profile, so guidance changes with the season instead of relying on one flat number all year.

Cards you can trust

The exported Lovelace Manual-card YAML shows what HI saw, mapped, skipped, and chose. These cards are there to make the routine easier to understand, not to invent their own logic.
