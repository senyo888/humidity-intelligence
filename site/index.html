01 / The integration

About Humidity Intelligence

Humidity Intelligence is a Home Assistant integration for managing humidity and air quality. It uses your configured room sensors to assess conditions, including condensation and mould risk, and coordinate ventilation, air purifiers, humidifiers and dehumidifiers.

Its control decisions follow defined priorities and the active humidity profile. The dashboard brings readings, device status and the reason for each response together, making the system straightforward to understand and troubleshoot.

02 / The bigger picture

One reading is a moment.
Stability tells you more.

A comfortable reading can hide recurring humidity imbalance, moisture risk or slow recovery. Stability Score puts that reading in context by showing how conditions have varied over time.

How the score, history and display work

Stability is observational: it does not select control lanes or write outputs. Existing priority rules determine the control response. The score requires sufficient valid history and usable current evidence. Restarting or reloading the integration clears that history and starts collection again.

The badge's recent-change arc compares the current score with a reference from within the past 60 minutes. This is separate from the 72-hour score and the history recorded at ten-minute intervals. LED colour reflects the most recent update, which may differ from the overall direction shown by the arc.

Press and hold the Stability badge to display Disabled; repeat to restore the normal display. This saved preference affects presentation only: calculation, history and control continue. Each environmental badge uses its own recorded source.

03 / Everyday control

Control for
changing conditions.

Cooking, showers and indoor laundry affect rooms differently. HI assesses those changes against your active humidity targets and coordinates the devices you have configured.

01

Defined control priorities

A fixed priority order selects one ventilation lane per evaluation cycle. Humidification follows its own demand, while the reason panel explains the selected response.

02

Seasonal and custom targets

Room conditions are assessed against the active humidity profile. Auto follows the seasons; you can also select a season or set custom targets.

03

Visible decisions and device status

The dashboard shows the readings and evidence behind each response. Optional output monitoring reports configured device states and identifies gaps in monitoring.

HI relies on the sensors and devices you configure and complements appropriate ventilation, heating and maintenance. A device reported as On in Home Assistant is not proof of physical airflow or moisture production.

v2.0.12 published Stable

A focused maintenance release.

Published Stable v2.0.12 improves Manual-mode handover, scheduled gates using Home Assistant local time, and timer countdowns. Lane priority and generated cards are unchanged.

Restart Home Assistant after updating. Stable v2.0.12 requires no configuration migration or re-export of Manual cards. A later authorised V2.1 update requires regenerating and replacing saved cards, followed by a frontend refresh. Restarting clears Stability history.
