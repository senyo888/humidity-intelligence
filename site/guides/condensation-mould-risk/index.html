Humidity Intelligence / Guides

Read the risk in context.

Condensation and mould-risk monitoring in Home Assistant

A house average can conceal a room that repeatedly stays damp. Humidity Intelligence brings moisture-risk readings, room context and control decisions into the same view, so recurring patterns are easier to investigate.

Look beyond the house average

Rooms experience different moisture loads and temperature changes. A bathroom after showers and a cooler bedroom can need separate attention even when the house average appears settled. HI uses configured room and level mappings to make the source of an alert traceable where that context is available.

Read the affected room, its humidity and temperature, the active target profile and the reported risk together. An isolated reading and a recurring pattern call for different investigation; neither should disappear behind an encouraging whole-house score.

Follow the selected control response

HI places humidity danger, mould and condensation lanes above routine zone and air-quality demand in its ventilation priority order. CO emergency retains the highest priority. For zone-bound moisture responses, the engine must resolve the source, room and zone; missing context cannot be replaced by an invented mapping.

The reason panel explains which response was selected and why. If you expected a different device to run, inspect that explanation, any active gate and the output mapping before changing thresholds. The control-decisions manual describes the full priority order.

Use the readings to investigate recurring patterns

Compare the affected rooms before and after familiar moisture-producing activities. Check whether readings return towards the active target, whether the same room remains elevated and whether the configured device reports the expected state. Sensor placement, missing readings and a device that does not physically operate can all limit the interpretation.

HI reports risk from configured evidence. It cannot inspect hidden surfaces, diagnose the cause of damp or confirm the absence of mould. Recurring dampness still needs investigation of the property, its ventilation and maintenance.

Add a longer view without losing current alerts

Stability Score includes moisture risk, target adherence, room balance and recovery across a rolling 72-hour window. Its details expose deductions and available history, helping you distinguish a brief excursion from repeated imbalance.

Missing evidence can restrict or suppress the score. A strong score does not clear an active alert or confirm an output is working. Keep current conditions, the reason panel and device observations alongside it.

For the broader environmental context, read why environmental stability matters. For sensor and room assignments, use the configuration walkthrough.
