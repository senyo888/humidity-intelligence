# v2.1.3 — Air Quality in Focus

[![Latest Release](https://img.shields.io/github/v/release/senyo888/Humidity-Intelligence?display_name=tag&sort=semver)](https://github.com/senyo888/Humidity-Intelligence/releases) [![HACS — Available in HACS](https://img.shields.io/badge/HACS-Available%20in%20HACS-41BDF5?logo=home-assistant&logoColor=white)](https://my.home-assistant.io/redirect/hacs_repository/?owner=senyo888&repository=humidity-intelligence&category=integration)

**Stable release preparation.** Mobile and Tablet advance to revision 27 from revision 26 in the final RC. At the preparation checkpoint the published Stable release was [v2.1.2](https://github.com/senyo888/humidity-intelligence/releases/tag/v2.1.2).

One monitor reports a VOC index. Another measures concentration in parts per billion. On Mobile and Tablet, you can follow each monitor’s index alongside the house concentration average, or choose which readings you want to see. Open the AQ badge for a timestamped snapshot that stays in place as fresh readings arrive.

## Air quality, monitor by monitor

In **Settings → Sensors**, add a monitor under **VOC index (display only)** and give it a name you’ll recognise on the card. Each index appears separately, on the scale its monitor reports. HI keeps the existing VOC concentration mappings and their units. The indices are display-only. Ventilation and the Stability Score continue to use their existing inputs.

When you have both types, open the **AQ** badge and choose **All configured readings**, **Monitor indices only** or **Concentration averages only**. Your choice follows your Home Assistant account across the Mobile and Tablet cards for that HI installation. If a connection problem interrupts a change, **Retry read** fetches the current preference and **Resend** sends your chosen setting again.

Complete AQ averages show their values and units in HI’s green, amber or red display bands. Screen readers announce the range as well. A configured sensor that goes quiet stays visible, and an incomplete average stays neutral. If Diagnostics goes offline, a verified reading from a mapped HI aggregate stays on the card alongside a configuration warning. A monitor’s VOC index keeps its own neutral colour. House averages include valid configured readings without a floor assignment. Floor averages use the readings assigned to that floor.

## A history you can follow

An operating mode carries the same colour from the current chip to the history ribbon and its legend. Current summaries use the same mode name and reason as the main card. An active alert or CO emergency still takes priority on the live card. Mobile and Tablet now both show red condensation and mould **Risk** icons. If the risk source goes offline, the card shows that loss of information. A previously reported “OK” or reason can no longer mask it.

Open a summary and its capture time tells you exactly when the snapshot was taken. Reopen it for a fresh reading. In history, the selected range, inspected point and scroll position stay in place as telemetry arrives. Historical reasons come from the recorded sample. The revision indicator also takes up less vertical space while keeping its 44-pixel tap target.

## Settings that keep related choices together

**Humidity targets and temperature comfort** puts the control range and the dashboard comfort range on one screen, with each purpose labelled. Sensor mappings live under **Sensors**, zone settings under **Zones**, and AQ thresholds under **Air quality control**. Enabling a zone fills in missing thresholds for its selected triggers. Enabling an AQ lane with missing thresholds opens a confirmation step before you save. If HI cannot read the saved AQ threshold collection, the lane can stay disabled while you review it. Unreadable VOC index entries are preserved when editing other sensors. Choose **Save changes** on the main settings page when you’re ready to apply your edits.

## Updating and rollback

1. Back up the existing HI package, settings and saved cards. After installing the complete update, restart Home Assistant Core.
2. Refresh the HI mappings and export each V2 layout you use. Replace the complete saved Mobile or Tablet card if it is **revision 26 or earlier**, including a card saved from the final RC. Both new layouts are **revision 27**. Existing concentration mappings carry forward.
3. To return to v2.1.2 after configuring VOC indices, clear only the display-only index sources in the newer version and save. Verify that the concentration mappings are still present, then restore the previous package, restart Core and restore its matching cards. A restart resets in-memory Stability history. [Compatibility and rollback guide](https://github.com/senyo888/humidity-intelligence/blob/d40b4e33e57ab749554919c93f3c6df7ff0830a0/docs/releases/v2.1.3-rc.1.md)

## A note from Senyo

Since v2.1.2, I’ve made a few visual upgrades to the HI dashboard. On Mobile and Tablet, you can now choose to show each monitor’s VOC index, the VOC concentration averages, or both. Each operating mode now has the same colour on the live card and in history. If a risk sensor goes offline, the card shows it as unavailable instead of leaving the old “OK” on screen.

I’ve also put humidity targets and temperature comfort together in Settings. The air-quality details stay open while the readings update, and if you’re looking through history, you keep your place.
